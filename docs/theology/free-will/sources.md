## Confessional evaluation

The [Reformed evaluation](reformed-evaluation.md#sources-and-further-study) identifies which sources control its constructive claims and which opposing primary or authoritative sources control its representations of alternative positions.

## Citation policy

Claims about doctrine use primary or official sources wherever possible. Modern philosophical classifications use “-leaning,” “analogous,” “commonly,” or “disputed” where the source does not adopt the label. No unverified quotations or page numbers are used; items without stable public editions are cited by work rather than supplied with an invented URL.
