# Comparison Matrix

> **Descriptive:** These matrices compare positions without endorsing one. The explicitly confessional evaluation is linked near the end.

These tables are maps into fuller pages, not substitutes for them. “Approximate category” identifies resemblance and must be read with the qualification column.

## Traditions across all seventeen dimensions

These three tables use the canonical dimensions without merging any of them. Each linked tradition page explains the biblical rationale and internal diversity. Terms are deliberately compact: **AP** means categorical alternative possibilities; **RR** means reasons-responsiveness; **gen./eff.** distinguishes general from effectual call.

## Confessional Reformed evaluation

The matrices above remain descriptive. For an explicitly non-neutral judgment of libertarianism, Arminianism, Wesleyanism, Molinism, Catholic Thomism, Eastern Orthodox synergy, Lutheranism, hard determinism, open theism, and Reformed compatibilism—including the Reformed model's own hardest objections—see the [comparative explanatory-cost table](reformed-evaluation.md#comparative-explanatory-costs).

## Reading the matrix responsibly

1. “Resistible” always needs an object: outward call, sufficient grace, effectual grace, or continuing grace.
2. “Synergistic” does not mean unaided or meritorious self-salvation.
3. “Compatibilist” does not mean fatalist or coercionist.
4. Election is not identical to foreknowledge or generic causal determinism.
5. Confessions rarely endorse a modern analytic theory by name.
